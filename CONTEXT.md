# Domain language

- **Workspace (organization / tenant):** an independent place for a group's projects, tasks, teams and membership. A person may belong to more than one workspace. Switching workspaces never transfers their records.
- **Membership:** a person's access and role in one workspace. Workspace ownership does not grant access to another workspace.
- **Project:** a collection of related tasks inside one workspace.
- **Task:** work belonging to one project, with status, assignee, dates, comments and optional subtasks.
- **Team:** a group within one workspace; it is not a separate tenant.
- **Personal preference:** a person's language, appearance and navigation choices. Changing workspace does not change the person's language.
- **Demonstration workspace:** sample content available on this device before a cloud account is connected. It is separate from authenticated cloud workspaces.
